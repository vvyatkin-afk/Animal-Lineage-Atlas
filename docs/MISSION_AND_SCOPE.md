# Mission and scope

## Mission

Make documented animal family relationships and individual life histories easier to explore while preserving links to the source and the source's limits. The atlas is a public research aid, not an official studbook, a replacement for zoo records, or a complete census.

## Current expanded corpora

| Atlas | Included records | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Red panda | 1,554 animals in one canonical dataset, including the current 1,475-profile `wwoast/redpanda-lineage` export and the 83-animal curated Futa-family layer, with four reviewed identity crosswalks | The upstream community project has no explicit license in its pinned repository metadata/tree. Attribution, snapshot provenance, and that rights status are recorded. No photo bytes are imported; all red-panda media remains link-only. Seventy-one other near-match profiles remain separate pending identity evidence. |
| Polar bear | 64 named bears from public zoo histories and announcements | A zoo/captive-history sample, not a complete studbook. The 4,449-individual Western Hudson Bay Dryad pedigree and a separate 414-record Hudson Bay-region candidate were not imported because their published data-file requests returned HTTP 403; no wild research IDs are mixed with named zoo bears. |
| Hippopotamus | 85 animals: 60 common hippos and 25 pygmy hippos, with 78 supported relationships | *Hippopotamus amphibius* and *Choeropsis liberiensis* are separate taxa in one atlas. Public zoo records support the included animals; studbook summaries are context only, not permission to copy restricted individual rows. |

## Exclusions and open gaps

Unknown parents do not become synthetic animals. Unnamed births remain events. Animals are not connected based only on co-housing or expected breeding pairs. Unsupported translations, precise dates, destinations, and present-day living status remain absent or explicitly uncertain.

The polar-bear corpus omits rows from the two Dryad candidates while their published file routes deny anonymous access; no alternate route was used. It also omits records available only in restricted studbooks or unsupported secondary lists. The hippo atlas keeps its two taxa separate and does not copy keeper-requested studbook rows. All corpora can grow when an accessible source identifies an individual and directly supports the proposed claim or event.

## Maintenance rule

Add a record only with an auditable source, exact source page, and preserved date precision. Recheck time-sensitive status and source conflicts before a new release. Official studbook custodians remain authoritative for their records.
