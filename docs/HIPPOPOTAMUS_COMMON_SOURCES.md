# Common hippopotamus public-source research

Access date for all pages below: **2026-10-08**. This note accompanies the import bundle [`common-hippo.json`](../atlases/hippopotamus/imports/common-hippo.json). Each source record preserves the publisher, publication date when stated, access date, exact URL, and source-specific use note.

## Coverage and evidence boundary

The import contains selected named *Hippopotamus amphibius* from Cincinnati Zoo and San Diego Zoo in the United States; Prague, Budapest, and Ostrava in Europe; Kobe Oji, Tobe, and Tokuyama in Japan; Berlin; and the historical London Zoo record. Official zoo association and Hippo Specialist Group pages provide program-access context. Claims about names, sex, status, dates, births, deaths, transfers, and parentage link to their sources. Parentage edges are limited to relationships directly identified in a cited public source. Unknown fathers remain unassigned. Group housing or mating is not used to infer paternity.

Unnamed calves are represented only as sourced birth/death events when that preserves a directly documented event; no animal node or invented name is created for them. The dataset uses unknown or partial dates when sources do not give an exact date. It contains no animal photos, image files, or direct image URLs.

## Ostrava historical chapter and studbook exclusion

The Ostrava animal facts are drawn from Jan Pluháček’s historical chapter “Keeping the common hippopotamus at Ostrava Zoo” in Zoo Ostrava’s public 2009 annual report. The chapter includes a selected named-animal history and table. This import uses supported names, dates, parentage, transfers, and deaths from that public chapter. It omits every studbook number and unnamed table row. It does not assert that the report carries a reuse license.

The full European common-hippo studbook is a separate keeper-request resource described by the Hippo Specialist Group; it is not reproduced or treated as a public dataset here. The Japanese common-hippo studbook is likewise described as keeper-request access. The public historical chapter is distinguished from the restricted full studbook in both the source record and import limitations.

## Coverage limitations

- Selected named Hippopotamus amphibius from public zoo, conservation and research publications; not a complete census or pedigree.
- Parentage is included only where a cited source directly identifies the parent. Unknown fathers, unnamed calves, cohabitation and ambiguous counts do not create nodes or parent edges.
- Ostrava records come from a public historical chapter in its 2009 annual report. The full European studbook is separately keeper-requested; no studbook numbers or restricted records are copied, and no report reuse license is asserted.
- The Japanese common-hippo studbook is also keeper-requested. This import uses named public zoo pages and does not reproduce studbook records.
- Historical living status and incomplete dates remain unknown or approximate. No animal photos, image files or direct image URLs are included.
