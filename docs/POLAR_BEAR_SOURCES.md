# Polar-bear atlas source notes

Research and review date: **2026-10-08**. Existing source pages were first reviewed on 2026-10-07; the added zoo pages and Dryad terms were checked on 2026-10-08. The canonical dataset is [`atlases/polar-bear/atlas.json`](../atlases/polar-bear/atlas.json).

## Corpus and counting

The reviewed corpus contains 64 named polar bears, 58 biological parent links, 84 life events, 7 claims, 31 institution/place records, 28 source records, and five link-only media references. It preserves the original Tallinn Zoo (10 bears), Tierpark Berlin, and Prague Zoo (Bora family) material and adds 42 named zoo records from public records of Zoo Rostock, Toronto Zoo, Columbus Zoo and Aquarium, Tiergarten Nürnberg, and Prague Zoo. This remains a source-bounded zoo/captive-history sample, not a complete studbook. The Western Hudson Bay wild research pedigree and a broader 414-bear Hudson Bay-region candidate are separate research populations and have no records in this atlas because Dryad’s published download routes denied access.

## Population separation and coverage tiers

- **Tier A — open primary datasets (metadata/terms):** Dryad’s Western Hudson Bay dataset page and reuse terms state CC0. It reports 4,449 pedigree individuals from Western Hudson Bay (around Churchill, Manitoba), spanning 1966–2011; the public file stream returned HTTP 403 and the documented anonymous API download returned HTTP 401. Dryad’s broader Hudson Bay-region dataset (DOI [10.5061/dryad.1719f](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.1719f)) also states CC0 and reports 414 bears with parent-ID columns, but the published PED, MAP, and README links returned HTTP 403. Its regional scope is broader than Western Hudson Bay. Neither dataset contributes rows, research IDs, or parent edges to this zoo atlas. See [`POLAR_BEAR_WESTERN_HUDSON_BAY.md`](POLAR_BEAR_WESTERN_HUDSON_BAY.md).
- **Tier B — official zoo records:** The named records and parent edges in the atlas cite zoo histories, profiles, announcements, or zoo-produced publications listed above and in the original-source table. Each parent edge carries one or more source IDs that directly name the parent and offspring.
- **Tier C — peer-reviewed/publication supplemental data:** The 2016 Viengkone et al. article page lists an additional 17.1 MB DOCX file. Its contents and reuse license were not directly inspected, so it remains discovery-only and was not reused or ingested.
- **Tier D — community-curated public data:** No community-curated genealogy source is used for these polar-bear records.
- **Discovery-only:** The University of Manitoba repository thesis by Leah Jane Kathan (2024), [Quantitative Genetics of Polar Bear (Ursus maritimus) Behaviours](https://mspace.lib.umanitoba.ca/bitstreams/f120db8e-a2b5-4683-94c4-d549dff31070/download), describes an updated 4,634-animal pedigree in prose but provides no row-level list or separately licensed data file. The PDF states copyright © 2024 by Leah Kathan; it is not used as an import source. The PMC-listed supplement for the Viengkone et al. paper is also discovery-only because its contents and license were not inspected.

The population label `zoo_captive` here describes records compiled from zoo/captive-history sources. It does not merge a captive bear with any wild research ID. Same-name bears are distinct IDs: `polar-bear:nora` (Tallinn), `polar-bear:nora-columbus`, and `polar-bear:nora-prague-1942`; `polar-bear:aurora-toronto` and `polar-bear:aurora-columbus` are also separate. Unstated parents, including Juno’s, remain absent rather than becoming placeholder records. No restricted studbook tables were scraped, and no new photos or photo bytes were added.

## Evidence decisions

- **Tallinn:** The zoo page states that Franz and Vaida had Jasper (1995), Marta (1996), Mart (1998), and Friida (2002). It identifies Friida and Nord as Nora's and Aron's parents. Only birth years are published for these cubs, so the event dates use year precision. The page reports arrival years for Vaida (1987), Franz and Joosep (1988), and Nord (2009), and no exact transfer dates for Jasper, Marta, or Mart. These are approximate or unknown. Marta and Mart's receiving destination is only “China”; the atlas leaves the facility unidentified. The page gives no reliable sex for Joosep or the four first-generation cubs, so those values remain unknown.
- **Berlin parentage correction:** The May 2021 genetics release says Tonja's earlier identity documents were wrong: it identifies Tonja as the cub born to Simona and Vrangel on 2009-11-16, and says Wolodja has the same parents. This corrects the former assumption that their parents were unidentified. The source is German; the atlas preserves the zoo's Latin-script proper names and does not create translated aliases.
- **Berlin date conflict:** The 2021 genetics release chronology says Fritz died on 2017-03-03. Tierpark's March 2017 death announcement says he died Monday evening, and the zoo's later Wolodja article specifies 2017-03-06. The event uses 2017-03-06 with the contemporary reports; both reported values remain in claims, with the 2021 chronology marked disputed. The source conflict is visible in coverage notes.
- **Prague:** Prague Zoo states Bora was born in Vienna on 1986-11-20, moved to Dvůr Králové at age two, and arrived in Prague on 1997-09-10. The page does not support a calendar year for the Dvůr Králové move, so that date is unknown with her age retained in notes. It identifies Alík as Pú's father. Its English profile calls Berta Bora's daughter, says she was first called Albert, and refers to Alík as her father. Pú left for Riga in May 2001; Alík died in 2005. January 2024 Zoo Praha reporting supersedes the older profile's “still living” wording for Berta: she died in September 2023. The newer notices give Tom's exact birth, February 2009 arrival, exact move to Almaty on 2024-03-28, and death on 2024-05-04. The 2024 source also identifies his father Umca. The Bora profile does not establish Bora's current status, so it remains unknown.
- **Present status:** Deceased status is used only for animals with a reported death. Current status is otherwise unknown unless the live zoo profile explicitly presents the individual as living. Historical event pages do not establish the current status of all animals as of 2026.
- **Media and reuse:** The five media entries link to official pages whose alt text or captions identify Franz, Nora, Aron, Bora, or Tom. No direct image URL, image bytes, thumbnail, or locally stored photograph is included. Page text and images remain the property of their publishers; the atlas uses factual metadata and links only.

## Gaps and extension path

The corpus omits unnamed cubs, bears appearing only in inaccessible studbook material, and other zoos unless an open primary source identifies an individual and a usable event or relationship. It does not infer family links from co-housing or breeding-pair proximity. Continue by adding an official zoo or openly reusable studbook source, recording the exact page URL and access date, preserving its date precision, and adding each relationship only when the cited source explicitly supports it. Recheck current living status, especially Bora's, and the Berlin date conflict before a later release.
